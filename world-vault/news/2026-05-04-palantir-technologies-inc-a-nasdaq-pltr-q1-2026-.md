**Palantir’s Q1 2026 Blowout: Revenue and EPS Smash Estimates as Growth Accelerates to Record Levels**

Palantir Technologies Inc-A ([NASDAQ:PLTR](https://www.chartmill.com/stock/quote/PLTR/profile)) delivered a quarter that left little doubt about its trajectory, reporting first-quarter results that handily beat analyst expectations on both the top and bottom lines. The company posted its highest-ever year-over-year revenue growth rate, fueled by a massive acceleration in its U.S. business, and raised its full-year guidance significantly above prior consensus figures. The market’s reaction has been swift, with shares surging in after-hours trading following the release.

**Revenue and EPS Crush Consensus Estimates**

The headline numbers were striking. Palantir reported total revenue of $1.633 billion for the quarter ended March 31, 2026, crushing the analyst consensus estimate of approximately $1.569 billion. On a per-share basis, adjusted earnings came in at $0.33, well ahead of the $0.28 analysts had penciled in, representing a 17.5% beat versus the consensus.

Key financial highlights for the quarter include:

*   **Total Revenue:** $1.633 billion (up 85% year-over-year) vs. $1.569 billion estimated.
*   **Adjusted EPS:** $0.33 vs. $0.28 estimated.
*   **U.S. Revenue Growth:** $1.282 billion, up 104% year-over-year.
*   **GAAP Net Income:** $871 million, delivering a 53% net income margin.
*   **Adjusted Free Cash Flow:** $925 million, or a 57% margin.
*   **Rule of 40 Score:** A stunning 145%, placing the company alongside top AI infrastructure peers.

The company’s performance was driven by broad-based strength across both government and commercial verticals. U.S. government revenue alone grew 84% year-over-year to $687 million, while U.S. commercial revenue skyrocketed 133% to $595 million. Palantir also reported total contract value (TCV) of $2.41 billion, up 61% year-over-year, and closed 206 deals worth at least $1 million during the quarter.

**Market Reaction: A Strong Vote of Confidence**

The market has responded emphatically to the news. Following the earnings release, shares of PLTR rose approximately 1.49% in after-hours trading. While the move may appear modest in percentage terms on the surface, it should be viewed in the context of the stock’s recent consolidation. The stock had declined roughly 3% over the past month and roughly 1.6% over the past two weeks, suggesting some nervousness heading into the print. The positive after-hours move signals that investors are breathing a sigh of relief and are willing to pay up for the accelerated growth trajectory.

This reaction is consistent with what we have seen from high-growth software names that successfully balance expanding margins with accelerating revenue. The market is digesting a narrative where Palantir is no longer just a story about long-term potential, but one of immediate, powerful operational leverage.

**Outlook: Guidance Raised Well Above Estimates**

Beyond the quarterly beat, the most significant catalyst for the stock was the sharp upward revision to forward guidance. Management raised its full-year 2026 revenue forecast to between $7.650 billion and $7.662 billion, representing approximately 71% year-over-year growth. This is a material increase from the prior guidance and sits well above the analyst consensus of $7.414 billion.

Key elements of the new outlook include:

*   **Full-Year 2026 Revenue:** Raised to $7.65–$7.66 billion (consensus was $7.41 billion).
*   **U.S. Commercial Revenue Guidance:** Raised to at least $3.224 billion, implying a growth rate of at least 120%.
*   **Full-Year Adjusted Income from Operations:** Raised to $4.44–$4.45 billion.
*   **Q2 2026 Revenue Guidance:** Set at $1.797–$1.801 billion, significantly above the analyst estimate of $1.714 billion.

This aggressive upward revision, particularly the ten-point increase in full-year growth guidance compared to just one quarter ago, reflects management’s confidence in an accelerating U.S. market. CEO Alex Karp pointed to the company's "Rule of 40 score soaring to 145%," a metric that now rivals only a handful of AI infrastructure leaders.

**Key Takeaways from the Press Release**

The earnings release contained several standout metrics that underscore the shift in the company's operational profile:

*   **Margin Expansion:** Adjusted income from operations hit a 60% margin, up from 44% in the year-ago quarter, demonstrating that growth is not coming at the expense of profitability.
*   **Cash Generation:** The company generated $899 million in cash from operations and $925 million in adjusted free cash flow, reflecting a 57% free cash flow margin.
*   **Strong Backlog:** U.S. commercial remaining deal value (RDV) hit $4.92 billion, up 112% year-over-year, providing strong visibility into future revenue.
*   **Customer Momentum:** Palantir closed 47 deals worth at least $10 million in a single quarter, a clear sign of deepening enterprise adoption.
