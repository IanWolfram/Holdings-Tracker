# Exclusive-Prices of Nvidia's B300 server at $1 million in China on US curbs, sources say

**Verdict**: BUY (60% confidence)  
**Relevance to [[NVDA]]**: 60%  
**Geographic origin**: US  

## Summary
Strong demand for AI computing equipment in China has nearly doubled prices for Nvidia's B300 servers to about 7 million yuan ($1 million) each, industry sources said, as a crackdown ‌on chip smuggling dries up black-market supply.  Prices of Nvidia's most advanced and powerful server, critical for artificial intelligence tasks, have ‌climbed since early this year, but rose sharply after the grey market, a key supply channel, came under pressure, the four sources said.  The price surge is also being ​driven by robust computing demand from Chinese technology companies, even as many avoid holding Nvidia hardware directly on their books for fear of exposure to U.S. sanctions, the sources said.
