How Alphabet Misrepresents Gemini Engagement &amp; Misleads Shareholders

**Reality: Google is paying OEMs like Samsung and Motorola not just to automatically distribute the gemini app on all of its phones, but change the default behavior of the power button to open the Gemini app(rather than locking the screen)**. Assuming Google uses the same methodology they use for Google Play Console, **anyone that pressed their lock screen button on an affected Android Device, which automatically opens Gemini, is counted as a monthly active user**
