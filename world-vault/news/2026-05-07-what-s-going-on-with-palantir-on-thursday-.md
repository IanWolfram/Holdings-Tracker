## Summary
Palantir Technologies (PLTR) stock rose 3.52% on Thursday following announcements of a defense collaboration with the U.S. Army and raised full-year 2026 revenue guidance from $7.18-7.20B to $7.65-7.66B, significantly exceeding analyst estimates of $7.27B. The company also beat Q1 earnings expectations with revenue of $1.63B versus $1.54B estimated and adjusted EPS of $0.33 versus $0.28 estimated. Multiple analysts raised price targets, with Rosenblatt increasing to $225 and Piper Sandler to $230, citing strong government business growth of 84% year-over-year.
