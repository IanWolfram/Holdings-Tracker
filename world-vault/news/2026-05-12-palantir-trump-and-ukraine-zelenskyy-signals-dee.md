# Palantir, Trump And Ukraine: Zelenskyy Signals Deeper Defense Tech Ties

## Summary
Ukrainian President Zelenskyy met with Palantir CEO Alex Karp to discuss deeper cooperation in defense technology. Palantir's software has been deployed on Ukrainian battlefields since 2022 for intelligence, targeting, and data analysis. The meeting positions Palantir as a key player in Western defense technology amid Trump's administration focus on military spending and Ukraine policy. Ukraine's Defense Minister noted the partnership has provided technological advantages including AI reconnaissance tools and a platform for training AI models with over 100 participating companies.
